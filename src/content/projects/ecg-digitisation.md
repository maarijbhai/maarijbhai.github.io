## PROBLEM

An ECG printout carries the whole signal on the page, but it isn't data. It can't be measured precisely, compared against a recording from six months ago, or passed to anything automated. Getting it back into numerical form means specialist software on a desktop, which is exactly what a clinic without a cardiology department doesn't have.

The interesting version of the problem is doing it from a phone photograph: not a flatbed scan, but a picture taken at an angle, under fluorescent light, on paper that may be creased.
