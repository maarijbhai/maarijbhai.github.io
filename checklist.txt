         Rename to  <new-slug>.md
[ ] 6.2  Fill the same fields as Stage 3
[ ] 6.3  Give it a unique  order:  number
[ ] 6.4  Create folder  src/assets/projects/<new-slug>/
         and drop a hero image in
[ ] 6.5  Point the  hero:  field there

         prev/next chain, and sitemap
